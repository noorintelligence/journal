Today I launched the first project in my daily build challenge: **Executive Problem De-Fugifier**.

### 🛠️ What I Built
A browser-native tool that converts raw business problems into structured **C-R-E-A-T-E** meta-prompts, inferring underlying business assumptions and diagnostic KPI triads (Volume, Value, Retention).

### 💡 Key Technical Takeaways
- **Zero-Backend Architecture:** Runs 100% client-side with JavaScript and Tailwind CSS.
- **Local Key Storage:** API keys are stored securely in browser `localStorage`.
- **API Fallbacks:** Configured for both Google Gemini and OpenAI REST endpoints.
